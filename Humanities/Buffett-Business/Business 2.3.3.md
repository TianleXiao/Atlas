## Leadership Styles: Autocratic
![[b2998ef8e9576c0f489de135d501964d.jpg]]

Autocratic leadership style is suitable –

- where quick decisions are needed and the scope for discussion must be limited. E.g., defense force.
- in times of crisis when decisive action might be needed to limit damage to business or danger to others.
- when the workers are unskilled so depend on the directions and instructions of leaders.

## Leadership Styles: Paternalistic
![[610831cfadabc411583caac38b2b6da4.jpg]]

- Paternalistic leadership is a leadership style where the manager or leader makes decisions on behalf of his/her team in the belief that these are in the best interest of the workers.
- Some consultation might take place, but final decisions are taken by managers.

---
## Leadership Styles: Paternalistic
![[c6c029c59ad402a2ba8af6de486719eb.jpg]]

Paternalistic leaders see employees as family members, and do what they think is the best for the workers.

- This style of leadership has worked well in countries such as Japan and India where the cultural setting has enabled people to work hard out of gratitude to their leaders.
- When workers are young or inexperienced this might be an appropriate style to employ.

Advantages:

- Motivates staff as they feel guided and that their interests are protected → promotes loyalty to the leader and the organization
- Creates a sense of belonging → Improves staff morale, motivation and productivity.

Disadvantages:

- Decision-making is still centralized, so workers can be dissatisfied if their views are ignored.
- Mainly top-down communication, from senior management.
- Paternalistic leaders may not always make the best decisions → can lead to conflict and disagreement.

## Leadership Styles: Democratic
![[ed32a5bfa114d381f548eb0c4bee73c6.jpg]]

- A democratic leader is one who is willing to delegate authority and consult subordinates in decision-making.
- This leadership style encourages all employees to share their views and ideas in the decision-making process. (Two-way-feedback is encouraged)
