## Direct Mail
Direct mail refers to the use of postal correspondence for promotional purposes. Examples include the use of newsletters, letters, postcards, brochures and catalogues sent through the post to targeted customers.
**Advantages—**
It helps to keep customers informed and to persuade them to make purchases.
It is used to target specific market segments and aims to encourage customers to buy directly from the business organization.
**Disadvantages—**
Potentially high costs of administration and postage.
Many customers regard direct mail as junk mail (spam) so don't pay attention to it and disregard it altogether.
The sheer amount of promotional material create advertising clutter, thus making it difficult for a particular advert to stand out.

## Public Relations
Public relations activities include: Publicity events; Speeches to targeted groups and professional organizations (such as seminars and receptions); Talk show appearances (radio or TV); Books and other writings; Direct communication (such as newsletters or email); Blogs and vlogs; Social media, such as Twitter, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, and Facebook; Working with the Press/ news media.
**Advantages—**
It helps to develop positive relationships with the press, media and general public.
It is highly cost effective if the news media provides free coverage of a positive story about the business
**Disadvantages—**
Journalists may report an amended version of the story.
Despite the efforts (time and money) needed to put a good story together for the press, there is no assurance that they will run the story.

## Sponsorship
Sponsorship is a BTL promotional strategy that involves a business providing financial support (the sponsor) to another organization or event. For example, Adidas sponsors the FIFA World Cup, and Coca-Cola sponsors the Olympic Games. Arsenal Football Club received a €132 million for a 15-year sponsorship deal in 2006 with Emirates Airlines.
**Advantages—**
There can be huge marketing advantages for the sponsor of being associated with the sponsored event or organization.
Televised broadcasts of these sporting events give businesses and brands exposure to a global audience.
**Disadvantages—**
Sponsorship deals can be very expensive (E.g. in football)
Negative behaviour of the sponsor could harm the organization's reputation. E.g. Volkswagen's heavy investment in football sponsorship deals became questionable after its emissions scandal in late 2015.
