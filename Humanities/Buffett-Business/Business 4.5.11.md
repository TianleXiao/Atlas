## Competitive Pricing Advs
- It is a low risk pricing strategy as the possibility of making a mistake with pricing is minimized. It helps to ensure a firm's prices are not too high or too low from the average market price.
- It is one of the simplest pricing methods, especially for new and small businesses. It simply requires the business to determine who their main competitors are and collect the prices charged by these firms before making a final pricing decision.
- There is flexibility as the business can make changes to prices to accommodate changes in the industry.
