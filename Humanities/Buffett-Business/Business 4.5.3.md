## The relationship between the plc and the marketing mix

### Growth
- Sales revenue increases as marketing efforts make the product and brand known to the market. The rising star is likely to be earning profit.

- The larger customer base warrants a larger number of distribution channels.

- The marketing mix focuses on improving the product, brand preference and recognition (with the aim of getting potential customers to switch from competing brands) and customer loyalty.

- Pricing strategy may need to change due to competition in the market and to facilitate continued growth.

- Investment costs will be high (especially for marketing), profit will materialize, and cash flow will be positive as sales grow steadily.

## The relationship between the plc and the marketing mix
### Maturity
- The marketing mix focuses on keeping the product as a cash cow for as long as possible in order to maximize and stabilize profits.
- Brand loyalty and brand value are vital aspects of product differentiation in order to prolong this stage of the life cycle.
- There is also price competition as the product competes in a mature market with well-established suppliers.
- Extension strategies may be required in order to lengthen the product's life cycle. Examples include adding new features to the product or launching a limited edition to spark renewed interest.
- Investment costs will be low as the product becomes well-established and mature, profit will peak, and cash flow will remain positive.
