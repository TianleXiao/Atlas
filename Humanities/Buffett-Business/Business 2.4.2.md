### Taylor's Scientific Management Theory – How to Improve Output per Worker
![[68eda436461c467d8252f4f293845e61.jpg]]

How to improve output per worker—
- Select the right people for each job
- Observe and record the performance of staff
- Record the time taken to do each part of the task
- Establish the best method of doing a job (method study)
- Supervise workers to ensure that this 'best way' is being carried out
- Pay workers on the basis of the results.


### Taylor's Approach: Limitations / Relevance to Modern Industry
![[0aa992f478ff358ff5bc1c7286d9bf84.jpg]]

- The more general view today is that workers have a wide range of needs.
- Piece-rate payment system is now not widely used.
- Quality may be sacrificed in the search for quantity.
- Workers may be discouraged from accepting changes at work in case they lose some pay.
- In most of modern industry, especially service industries, it has become very difficult to identify the output of individual workers.

### Maslow's Hierarchy (1943)

Maslow's hierarchy of needs: The 5S model
Self-actualisation
Self-esteem
Social needs
Security needs
Survival needs

Self-actualization (reaching full potential)
Esteem needs (respect from others, recognition for well done job, status)
Social needs (friendship accepted, belonging to a group)
Safety needs (health and safety at work, job security, free from threats)
Physical needs (water, food, shelter, clothing, and rest)
