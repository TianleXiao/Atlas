## Internal Recruitment
![[20261123_134353.jpg]]
Internal recruitment

Internal recruitment is the process of filling out a vacant post with someone already employed in the business.

Advantages:
- The vacancy can be filled out quickly and cheaply.
- Applicants already know how the business works.
- The business already knows the strengths and weaknesses of the applicants.
- Workers can become more motivated when they see that there is a chance of promotion.

Disadvantages:
- There will still be a vacancy to fill, unless the worker's previous job has become redundant.
- It does not bring in any new ideas.
- A better candidate may be available from outside the business.
- It could cause conflict in the workplace if other internal candidates feel they should have the job.

## External Recruitment
![[20261123_134947.jpg]]
External recruitment

Methods include:
- Specialist trade publications
- Internet advertising
- Job centres
- Headhunting
- University visits
- Employee referrals

Advantages:
- Brings new ideas and fresh experience into the business.
- Widens the field of candidates, so a better person may be appointed.
- Avoids the resentment that unsuccessful internal candidates may feel.

Disadvantages:
- It takes longer time to fill the vacancy.
- It is more expensive because of advertising costs and the time spent interviewing candidates.
- External applicants will need induction training, which increases expenses.

## Financial Rewards: Hourly Wage Rate
![[20261123_135607.jpg]]
Financial rewards: hourly wage rate

Hourly wage rate / time rate: pay per hour.

A common method of paying production workers and non-managerial staff.

Advantage: the business only pays workers for the number of hours they are at work.

Disadvantage: worker's pay is not linked to how much they produce.

Overtime rate is an hourly wage rate for the hours they work in excess of their contracted hours.

It is common for overtime to be paid at 1.5 times the pay (50% extra) or double pay (100% extra) to compensate the employee for sacrificing more leisure time.
