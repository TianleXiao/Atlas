## Vroom's Expectancy Theory of Motivation
![[c6e445ce8142d9428a0d3fd048922f7e.jpg]]
Vroom's Expectancy Theory of Motivation (1964)

M = E x I x V

- It highlights the interplay of Expectancy, Instrumentality, and Valence in determining motivation.
- If any of these elements is too low or zero, overall motivation diminishes.
- Vroom advocates establishing positive links between high effort and appropriate rewards to generate high performance, emphasizing that all three elements should be sufficiently high for effective motivation.

Expectancy x Instrumentality x Valence = Motivation

- Effort: If I work hard, will I hit my targets?
- Performance: If I hit my targets, will I be rewarded?
- Reward: Do I find the rewards desirable?

## Employee Turnover: Causes and Costs
![[38bab8c8fb9565714cb7a70c6f2010c6.jpg]]
Employee turnover

Beyond retaining talented and capable workers, businesses should take a keen interest in the recruitment process in employee retention, if only to avoid the staggering costs of employee turnover.

Causes of employee turnover:
- Inducting and training new staff
- Recruiting staff (advertising, interviewing, selection)
- Getting workers familiar with the operations and corporate culture
- Lower productivity while the new workforce settles in
- Negative publicity / reputation damage, as it shows the business struggles to retain its workforce

## Performance Appraisal and Its Objectives
![[e1d7642a10933738d0ac0223b21e308e.jpg]]
Appraisal

Performance management is a continuous process involving the planning, monitoring, reviewing and mentoring of employees in order to enhance their performance at work.

Managers will use a range of data, information, meetings and interviews to assess each employee.

The appraisal is the regular review of an employee's job performance and overall contribution to a company.

Appraisal: assessment of an individual's performance in line with his/her job description and the tasks and responsibilities set in their job description.

## Appraisal: Objectives and Outcomes
![[e5114240218210d172ffda1f302ac34b.jpg]]
Appraisal objectives:
- Assess and record an employee's performance in line with his/her job description and targets.
- Assist staff in reflecting on their performance at work.
- Provide an opportunity to praise staff for their good work.
- Identify any barriers hindering the performance of an employee.
- Identify appropriate training and development needs of the appraisee.
- Set new targets and goals for continuous improvement to aid professional development.
- Help employees to plan their careers and aid management in assessing the suitability of individuals for a pay rise or promotion.
