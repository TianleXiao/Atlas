### Who's a Leader?
![[e2a5f04801d5d6ae2527a9b5dd3c3d6e.jpg]]

Leadership involves influencing people to achieve a shared vision or goal.
Leadership is the process of influencing and inspiring others to achieve organizational goals.

- Influence and Power: Both official and informal leaders have the power to influence others, although the source of power differs. Official leaders derive power from their organizational position, while informal leaders rely on personal qualities and charisma.
- Leadership Focus: Effective leaders concentrate on people, aiming to unite them toward a common goal or vision. They take calculated risks to ensure the organization's survival, success, and sustainability.

Leadership is Action not.. position

### What's a Good Leader?
![[d8920e5edcd934e96d84a48c974ed214.jpg]]

The traditional view of a 'good' leader
- A strong, decisive character.
- An 'expert' in the relevant field of business.
- An autocrat (to a significant degree).
- Focus on profits and financial success.
- Good communicator, but most comfortable with downward communication.

The modern view of a 'good' leader
- Charismatic, with a flair for public relations.
- Possessing principles; for example, making ethical decisions.
- Excellent communicator and a good listener.
- Welcoming advice and support from specialists.
- Flexible and able to flourish in a changing environment.

### Styles of Leadership
![[b2b9d188dc316f02cfbe00ed919b2882.jpg]]

- Autocratic — The leader makes all the decisions.
- Paternalistic — The leader makes decisions on behalf of his/her team.
- Democratic — Workers take part in decision-making.
- Laissez-faire — Most of the decisions are left to the workers.
- Situational — The leader is willing and able to change leadership style according to the situations being faced.

---

### Leadership Styles: Autocratic
![[b412e9bdda573cb4c0c2303480e203f7.jpg]]

- An autocratic leader is one who centralises decision-making without consulting others or listening to their points of view.
- One-way communication - from leader to worker, no opportunity for feedback.

Advantages:
- Leader has complete control of the operations.
- Speeds up the business decision-making process, especially for critical and urgent decisions.
- Clear directions and instructions to workers

Disadvantages:
- Creativity and innovation are suppressed
- It does not develop talents of the workforce
- It demotivates employees as their opinions and suggestions are not valued.
- Higher labour turnover rate, which is costly to any organization.

Autocratic — I'm in control!
