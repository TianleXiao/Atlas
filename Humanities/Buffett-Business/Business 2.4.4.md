### Frederick Herzberg — Two Factor Theory (1959)
![[c4d04f32eabc4d65b0465d76fc1062cc.jpg]]

Herzberg's research identified two groups of factors that motivated people at work:
- Hygiene/ maintenance factors: are aspects of work that do not motivate but must be met to prevent dissatisfaction, essentially, they are factors that meet people's basic needs. For example:
  - pay must not fall below the level considered to be acceptable by the workforce.
  - relationship with colleagues and working conditions should be reasonable.
  - Hygiene factors become an expectation and are taken for granted, such as employer contributions to a worker's pension fund. Hence, maintenance factors do not motivate an employee to work any harder.
- Motivating factors (motivators): aspects of a worker's job that can lead to positive job satisfaction, such as achievement, recognition, meaningful and interesting work and advancement at work.

### Frederick Herzberg — Two Factor Theory: Hygiene Factors and Motivators
![[0b753e88ce491f1aac5b69d95c89e27f.jpg]]

| Hygiene factors | Motivators |
| --- | --- |
| Working conditions: clean & safe | The work itself (varied and challenging) |
| Relationship with others: friendship | Responsibility (trust and value) |
| Salary or wage: enough | Advancement (opportunity of promotion) |
| Supervision (leadership style) | Achievement (reach challenging goals) |
| Company policy and administration (rules and procedures): rules and procedures | Recognition of achievement |

- Factors surrounding the job (Hygiene factors)
- Factors about the job itself (Motivators)

### Herzberg's Two Factor Theory — Consequences for Today's Business
![[b1c23cd2a37ce8525bfaad1c1b119b3d.jpg]]

- Pay and working conditions can be improved and these will help to remove dissatisfaction about the work.
- Money gets people to do a job but does not motivate them to do well/ better.
- Motivators need to be put in place for workers to be prepared to work willingly and to always do their best.
- The work itself needs to be interesting, rewarding and challenging to motivate workers to offer their full potential.
