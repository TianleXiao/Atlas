## Managers vs Leaders
![[8618c552d2121bf7d07ea5eab2f7dc23.jpg]]

| Manager | Leaders |
| --- | --- |
| Do things right (achieve goals) | Do the right thing (sets goals) |
| Doing what's right | Knowing what's right |
| Asks how? | Asks why? |
| Directing and controlling others | Motivating and inspiring others |
| Delegating tasks to subordinates | Empowering 'followers' |
| Task orientated | People orientated |
| Conform to organizational norms | Create and foster a culture of change |
| Implementers | Innovators |
| Position | Action |
| Averts risks | Takes risks |
| Tactical planning and monitoring | Strategic decision-making |
| Listened to by others | Respected by others |
| Analysis | Decisiveness |
| Learned skills | Natural instincts |

## Effective Leadership Style
![[c2070723b29cdf793afab7aaa7474d39.jpg]]

Whether a leadership style will be effective will depend on several factors such as:
- The traits, personality and experiences of the manager or leader
- The level of skills, experience, motivation and confidence of the employees
- The time frame, i.e. how quickly decisions need to be made.
- The task, e.g. whether the task is routine or a response to a crisis.
- The degree of importance of the decision, e.g. tactical or strategic decision-making.

## Management vs Leadership
![[9ff67bbde8858764fe6c5e4924325279.jpg]]

Management
- Deal with tactical decisions (task-orientated)
- Deal with how and when questions (day-to-day problems)
- Give instructions and orders
- Follow predetermined rules and policies set by the organization → focus on accomplishing tasks
- Abide by the procedures and culture of an organization

Leadership
- Deal with strategic decisions (strategic direction of the organisation)
- Deal with what and why questions
- Inspire and motivate their followers through action
- Take risks by challenging the organizational norms to move the organization forward
- Create a culture of hope, getting people to where they haven't been before
